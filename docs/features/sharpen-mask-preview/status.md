## How it is built

- **PhotoCore, rendering** (`AdjustmentRenderer`). `sharpen` takes its
  neighborhood range and its Masking blend mask from two helpers
  (`SharpeningNeighborhood`, `maskingMask`); `sharpeningMask` calls the same
  two. The map is therefore the step's own mask, not a copy; the Core Image
  graph `sharpen` builds is unchanged. `downstream` is split at the step
  (`beforeSharpening`), and `sharpeningInput` is the image the step sees.
  `sharpeningMapDisplay` encodes mask *v* as the sRGB grey *v*, clamped to
  standard range.
- **PhotoCore, rules** (`SharpeningMap.swift`). `SharpeningMapStatus`: the
  not-applied rule (design §7, RI-1), read by the badge, legend, VoiceOver
  value, announcement and menu state. `SharpeningMapState`: suspend and
  restore (§6).
- **PhotoCore, frames.** `LiveRenderSession.render(content: .sharpeningMap)`
  renders the map through the same stage cache and drafts; map frames have
  no histogram, and `Frame.showsPhoto` decides what may give the histogram
  or seed the preview cache. `SharpeningMapRefiner` (an actor of its own,
  so a slow refine never queues live frames) renders the map from the
  full-resolution decode, keeping that image's sharpening input while only
  Sharpening changes, and scales it to the frame size at fit or keeps it
  whole when zoomed.
- **PhotoApp.** `AdjustmentEditor` owns the map: it renders map frames
  while it is on, refines after a 150 ms pause, shows the spinner when a
  refine is still running 0.5 s after the input, and frees the
  full-resolution input when the map goes. `LibraryModel.setSharpeningMap`
  is the one command path (menu, context menu, VoiceOver action, Esc,
  inspector toggle, debug script). The loupe hides mask handles under the
  map, so clicks pan and zoom and never paint.

## PRD Q5 (engineer): scale assumption and the cost of R6

Measured with `LargeSharpeningMapTests` on a synthetic 6000 × 4000 Bayer
DNG with noise 0.01 (no adjustments, Amount 60, Radius 1), Apple M4 Pro,
debug build, timed through reading the pixels back:

| Masking | Live frame, 2560 px (scale 0.43) | Refined, fit | Refined, 1:1 | Mean mask, centre: live / refined fit / refined 1:1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 0.12–0.13 s | 0.23–0.29 s (first: decode and input) | 0.16–0.26 s | 1.000 / 1.000 / 1.000 |
| 30 | 0.02–0.03 s | 0.16 s | 0.14–0.16 s | **0.504** / 0.927 / 0.930 |
| 60 | 0.02 s | 0.16–0.17 s | 0.14–0.16 s | **0.007** / 0.080 / 0.034 |

- **The assumption holds, strongly.** On a noisy flat area at Masking 30, the
  map at the loupe's fit scale lets half the sharpening through, while the
  full-size map (what an export sharpens) lets 93 % through. Resampling
  averages the noise away, so a reduced render's neighborhoods look smoother.
  This is the reason R6 matters, and it is also a **rendering issue in its
  own right**: the photo's own sharpening at fit (rendered at that scale)
  masks noise that a full-size export sharpens. Not fixed here (R13);
  suggested for a roadmap item.
- **R6's cost is acceptable** on this machine: about 0.25 s for the first
  refine of a photo, then about 0.16 s per pause. The synthetic DNG is
  uncompressed, so a real compressed 24 MP raw decodes more slowly; not
  measured (no real raw used). The refiner holds the full-resolution input,
  pixels × 8 bytes (192 MB at 24 MP), while the map is shown.
